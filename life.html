Life of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah

Birth

Muhammad Ali Jinnah was born on 25 December 1876 in Karachi. He belonged to a merchant family and was a very intelligent student from a young age.

Education

He received his early education at Sindh Madrasa-tul-Islam in Karachi. At the age of 16, he went to London for higher studies and joined Lincoln’s Inn to study law. He became a barrister in 1896 and returned to India to start his legal career as a successful lawyer in Bombay.

How He Created Pakistan

Muhammad Ali Jinnah became a great political leader and led the Muslim League. He supported the Two-Nation Theory, which said that Muslims and Hindus are two separate nations. He demanded a separate homeland for Muslims and worked through political struggle, speeches, and negotiations. In 1947, his efforts succeeded and Pakistan was created on 14 August 1947.

Death

In his final years, Jinnah suffered from serious illness including tuberculosis. Despite his weak health, he continued to serve Pakistan. He passed away on 11 September 1948 in Karachi at the age of 71.
